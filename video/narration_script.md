# Carbon Atlas India — Narration Script (for a human voiceover)

Read at a relaxed, documentary pace. Pause ~0.5s at commas, ~1s between sections.
The synthetic-voice video uses the same script; a human read of this is the definitive fix
for "the voice sounds fake."

## Cover
Ever wondered where a city's carbon actually comes from? This is Carbon Atlas India.
It watches a city breathe carbon, block by block, and then shows you how to clean it up.
Come on, let me walk you through it.

## Globe
We begin zoomed way out. See those glowing dots? Each one is an Indian city, and its size
and colour tell you how much carbon its roads push out every year. Red means heavy, teal
means light. Just click a city, and the atlas flies you straight down to street level.

## Earth View
Now we're over Delhi, on real satellite imagery. Every one of these rising columns is a
single city block. Taller and redder means more emissions in that month. Hit play, and watch
the whole year breathe in and out. The amber stars are the best spots for new chargers, and
the blue rings flag unusual days the model didn't expect.

## Predict
Curious about one exact spot? Drop a pin, and a real trained model runs right here in your
browser. It reads the season, the day of week, the air quality and the vehicle fleet, then
answers in tonnes of carbon per day. And it's honest about its confidence, too.

## Model Insights
But we don't just trust the model. We check it. These cards show how well it predicts on
places it has never seen, what actually drives emissions, and how closely emissions track
with air quality. Every number here is computed from real data.

## EV Optimizer
Now the part city planners care about. Slide the budget, and the atlas picks exactly where
new chargers cut the most carbon. It even tells you how much you save per site, checked
against real benchmarks from the pipeline.

## Roadmap 2030
Then it looks ahead to twenty thirty. Cleaner electricity plus more electric vehicles stack
up to cut roughly a third of road carbon. Move the sliders, and the future redraws itself
in front of you.

## Compare, Ask, Reports
Want to compare? Put any two cities side by side and watch the live difference in emissions,
blocks and charging coverage. You can also ask the atlas a question in plain language, or
export the whole session as a clean report.

## End
And that's Carbon Atlas India. Real data, a real model, and a clear path from seeing carbon
to clearing it. Go explore it live, and start with your own city.
